The Allstrong Thawing Tub is a passive thawing system designed for the controlled defrosting of frozen meat, poultry, and seafood products in commercial food-processing and central-kitchen environments. The unit utilizes water immersion as the primary heat-transfer medium, ensuring uniform thawing while maintaining product integrity and full food-safety compliance. NSF Listed (Standard 2) and UL Listed for the Motor.

Thawing Process

How It Works

01
🧊
Load Frozen Product
Place frozen meat, poultry, or seafood into the stainless steel #304 tub. The large 31"×31"×16" interior accommodates high-volume batches efficiently.
02
💧
Water Immersion
Water fills the tub, fully submerging the product. Water immersion is used as the primary heat-transfer medium for fast, controlled defrosting.
03
🌀
Vortex Pump Circulation
The ¾ hp High Pressure Vortex Pump continuously circulates water around the product, ensuring even temperature distribution and uniform thawing throughout.
04
✅
Safe & Compliant
Maintains product integrity and food-safety compliance throughout the process. NSF Standard 2 Listed for sanitation and UL Listed for motor safety.

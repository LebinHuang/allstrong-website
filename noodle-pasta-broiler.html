Engineering Highlights

Key Features

🔄
Instant Mode Switch
Converts from wok cooking to noodle/pasta boiling in seconds using a precision-fit adapter. No tools or gas system modifications required — maximum kitchen flexibility.
🍜
12 Baskets Simultaneously
9-hole cooking plate supports up to 12 noodle or pasta baskets at once — ideal for high-volume noodle restaurants, dim sum kitchens, and fast-paced service environments.
⚡
130,000 BTU Power
Single 23-Tips Jet Burner delivers 130,000 BTU/HR — powerful enough for rapid boil recovery even during the busiest service periods.
🎛️
Front-Panel Controls
Burner, faucet, and drain controls are all located on the front panel for quick, accessible operation during service. Ergonomically designed for the chef's workflow.
💧
Custom Water Tank
Integrated custom water tank built into the single-hole wok range. Provides consistent water supply for noodle cooking without the need for external tanks or extra plumbing.
⛽
Dual Gas Compatible
Available for both Natural Gas and Liquid Propane. ¾" gas regulator included. Easy to configure for any commercial kitchen gas setup in the USA or Canada.
📐
Compact Footprint
Compact 24"L × 41" × 50"H footprint packs full dual-function capability into a space-efficient station — perfect for kitchens where every inch counts.
✅
NSF & CSA Certified
Fully certified to NSF and CSA standards for commercial use in both the United States and Canada. Meets all health department requirements for professional kitchen installation.
ℹ️
Note: Pasta Strainer / Blanching Baskets Not Included The 9-hole plate and cooking platform are included. Pasta strainer and blanching baskets must be purchased separately. Contact us for compatible basket recommendations.

Engineering Highlights

Key Features

🔥
High-Output Burner System
Powerful duck-tip jet burners deliver consistent, high heat across the entire cooking surface. Two rings spaced equally for excellent combustion distribution and fast heat-up times.
🌡️
Anti-Warp ¾" Steel Top
The ¾"-thick steel grill plate endures surface temperatures up to 650°F without deformation. Superior heat transfer minimizes hot spots across the entire surface.
🛡️
304-Grade Stainless Steel
Exterior and construction use 304-grade stainless steel with 2" double-wall insulation. Outstanding resistance to heat, corrosion, and grease for long-term durability.
🧹
Integrated Grease Management
Built-in stainless steel scrap ring with 2½" flange and four galvanized grease drip pans at all quadrants. Easy daily cleaning and maintenance with minimal downtime.
👥
Interactive Open-Cook Design
Open cooking design enhances customer engagement and visual appeal — perfect for interactive dining concepts. Increases table turnover and creates a memorable dining experience.
🎛️
Manual Burner Control
Each burner has its own safety pilot control with standard pilot system. Thermal control is visible through a pre-cut window for easy monitoring during service.
⚖️
Adjustable Bullet Legs
Four adjustable and removable sanitary bullet-type legs for convenient leveling on uneven floors and easy relocation within the kitchen as needed.
🥩
Versatile Multi-Ingredient
Ideal for meats, vegetables, noodles and more. Designed for continuous multi-portion cooking in high-traffic environments — supports fast service speeds and high customer throughput.

Buying Guide

When the
WT-EE Is the
Right Choice

✅
Light to Moderate Workload
Cold prep, pastry, bakery, or assembly lines — tasks that don't involve constant heavy impact on the top surface.
✅
Dry Kitchen Environments
Non-wet stations away from dishwashers or steam equipment where galvanized base rust risk is minimal.
✅
Tight Budget or Startup
New restaurant buildouts, ghost kitchens, and pop-ups where ETL certification is required but budget is limited.
✅
Secondary Prep Stations
Back-of-house secondary tables — plating, staging, packaging — where the top isn't the primary heavy-use surface.
⚠️
Not for Wet Stations
If the table will sit next to a sink or dish area with constant moisture, consider the WT-P All-SS or WT-E series instead.
⚠️
Not for Heavy Impact Use
Butchering, heavy chopping, or placing cast iron directly on the top — the 18GA is thinner and will dent more easily under impact.

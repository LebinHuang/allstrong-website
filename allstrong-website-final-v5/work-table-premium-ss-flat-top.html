The Undershelf
Matters

Most budget work tables save costs by using galvanized metal for the undershelf and legs — which can rust in wet kitchens over time. This premium model uses stainless steel throughout for superior hygiene and longevity.

Construction Breakdown

All SS
w/ Undershelf
Anatomy

01
16-GA 304 SS Top — Polished
16-gauge 304-grade polished stainless steel top with welded-on hat channel reinforcement. Sound-deadened between top and channels. The heavier 16GA gauge resists denting and deflection under heavy commercial loads.
02
All-SS Undershelf — Adjustable
Stainless steel undershelf at adjustable height — no galvanized components below the top. Stores pans, containers, and supplies without rust risk even in wet kitchen environments. The defining advantage over the WT-E series.
03
SS Legs + Aluminum Bracket Support
Stainless steel legs throughout. Aluminum corner brackets at the leg-top junctions provide extra rigidity. Gussets recessed into the hat channel prevent lateral movement and racking over time.
04
Zero Galvanized — Full Corrosion Resistance
Every component — top, undershelf, legs, and gussets — is stainless steel. No galvanized parts that can eventually rust in wet environments. The right choice for stations near sinks, steamers, or dishwashers.
05
Rolled Edge + ETL Certified
All four edges of the top are rolled smooth — no sharp corners anywhere. ETL Certified for commercial use in the USA and Canada. Adjustable ABS bullet feet standard on all models. 72"+ have 6 legs as standard.

Engineering Breakdown

How It's Built

01
🏗️
16-Gauge 304 SS Top
Polished 16-gauge stainless steel top — heavy-duty gauge for lasting durability. Corrosion resistant, easy to sanitize, and professional looking for front-of-house prep visibility.
02
📦
All-SS Undershelf
18-gauge stainless steel undershelf at adjustable height — no galvanized rust risk. Store pans, containers, and supplies directly below the work surface. Keeps items off the floor.
03
🔩
SS Legs & Gussets
Stainless steel legs and gussets throughout — no mixed metals. Aluminum corner bracket support at leg-top junctions. Gussets recessed into the hat channel to minimize lateral movement.
04
🎩
Welded Hat Channel
Top reinforced with welded-on hat channel for rigidity. Sound-deadened between top and channels reduces noise during prep. Prevents top deflection under heavy loads.
05
✂️
Rolled Edge Construction
Smooth rolled-edge on all exposed table top edges. No sharp corners anywhere — prevents staff injuries and simplifies cleaning. Standard on all GSW premium-grade tables.
06
🦶
Adjustable ABS Feet
1" ABS adjustable bullet feet level the table on any uneven commercial kitchen floor. Corrosion-proof, easy to clean, and won't scratch tile, epoxy, or concrete flooring.

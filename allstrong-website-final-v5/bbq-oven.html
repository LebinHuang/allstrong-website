Engineering Highlights

Key Features

🔥
High-Temperature Capability
Engineered for extreme heat needed in Chinese-style whole-animal roasting. Reaches temperatures required for perfectly crispy skin on duck, goose, and suckling pig.
☀️
Direct Flame + Radiant Heat
Dual-heat system combines direct flame with radiant heat reflection. This unique combination produces the authentic crispy exterior and tender interior of traditional Chinese roasting.
🏗️
Heavy-Duty Steel Construction
Fully insulated walls and doors with heavy-duty steel frame. Stainless steel exterior on front, sides, and top. Interior finished in heavy-gauge heat-resistant steel.
🚿
Hygienic Easy-Clean Design
Full-size galvanized drip pan under each burner for grease and debris collection. Smooth stainless surfaces make daily cleaning fast and thorough.
🎛️
Precise Heat Control
Manually controlled single gas valve with standard pilot system. Optional thermostatic multi-burner control with safety pilot system available for precise temperature management.
🔄
Carousel & Fixed Bracket
Smaller EQ-24 and EQ-30 models feature Fixed Bracket hangers. Larger EQ-36 and EQ-48 models include rotating Carousel hangers for even roasting on all sides.
🔩
Reinforced Door & Hinges
Custom-reinforced stainless steel door with heavy-duty hinges. Adjustable legs for level installation on uneven kitchen floors. Choose Left or Right hinge at time of order.

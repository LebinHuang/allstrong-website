Built to Last Decades

Every Allstrong work table shares the same engineering DNA — built for daily commercial abuse.

🏗️
16-Gauge 304 SS
Heavy-duty 16-gauge 304-grade stainless steel — the gold standard for commercial kitchens. Resistant to corrosion, staining, and bacterial growth.
🔧
Welded Hat Channel
Top reinforced with welded-on hat channel framework. Sound-deadened between top and channels for quiet operation. Eliminates sagging under load.
⚓
Recessed Gussets
Gussets recessed into channel framework reduces lateral movement. Aluminum cross bracing on premium models for additional rigidity.
🦶
Adjustable Bullet Feet
ABS bullet-style adjustable feet level the table on uneven floors. Easy to clean, won't scratch flooring, prevent rocking and noise.
📏
Rolled Edge
Smooth rolled edge construction along all sides. Eliminates sharp corners, prevents injuries, and provides a finished professional appearance.
📦
Knock-Down Shipping
Shipped knock-down for cost-effective freight. Easy to assemble in minutes with included hardware. Minimizes shipping damage and storage space.
💪
6-Leg Reinforcement
All work tables 72" and longer come with 6 legs as standard for extra strength. Prevents sagging and supports heavy commercial workloads.
✅
ETL Certified
All Allstrong work tables are ETL Certified for safety and performance. Meets all applicable health department and code requirements.

Construction Breakdown

Practical
Protection
Built In

01
1½" Rear Upturn — Radius Intersection
The 1½" upturn rises from the back edge of the table top with a smooth radius where the two surfaces meet — no sharp angle, no gap where liquid can collect. Low-profile enough to look clean against any wall, functional enough to stop liquid from running off the back edge.
02
16-Gauge SS Top
Same 16-gauge stainless steel top as the commercial WT-E series — not the thinner 18GA economy grade. Welded-on hat channel underneath reinforces the top against flex and impact. Sound-deadened between top and channels for quieter operation.
03
Recessed Gussets
Gussets recessed into the hat channel eliminate lateral movement between the legs and the top structure. Prevents wobbling and racking over time — critical for a table placed against a wall where the rear legs may not be easily adjusted.
04
Galvanized Undershelf, Legs & Gussets
18-gauge galvanized undershelf at adjustable height — same as the WT-E commercial model. Galvanized steel base delivers structural strength and decent corrosion resistance at a cost-effective price point relative to all-SS construction.
05
6 Legs on 72"+ · ABS Bullet Feet
All models 72" and above ship with 6 legs as standard for extra support across the longer span. Adjustable ABS bullet feet level the table on uneven floors without scratching tile or concrete.

The Most Popular Configuration in Commercial Kitchens
The SS top + galvanized base combination is the industry standard. You get the hygienic, easy-to-clean stainless steel food-contact surface where it matters most — with the cost-effective galvanized steel base where rust risk is minimal. This is how the majority of commercial restaurant kitchens are equipped.

Allstrong WT-E Series Commercial Work Tables deliver quality S/S unsurpassed strength and durability at a practical price point. Polished 16-gauge stainless steel top reinforced with welded-on hat channel and sound-deadened between top and channels. Gussets recessed into channel to reduce lateral movement. Galvanized undershelf, legs and gussets with adjustable ABS bullet feet. Models 72" and above include 6 legs for extra strength. ETL Certified for commercial use in USA and Canada.

01
16-Gauge Polished SS Top
The food-contact surface is polished 16-gauge stainless steel — durable, corrosion-resistant, and easy to sanitize. The heavier 16GA gauge means less flex and longer service life than economy-grade 18GA tops.
02
Welded Hat Channel + Sound Deadening
The underside of the top is reinforced with a welded-on hat channel framework. Sound-deadening material is installed between the top and channels — absorbs vibration, reduces noise, and prevents the hollow "drum" sound during prep.
03
Recessed Gussets
Gussets recessed into the hat channel — not external, not visible. Reduces lateral movement between the legs and the top. Structural rigidity without sacrificing the clean, professional appearance.
04
Galvanized Undershelf, Legs & Gussets
All base components are galvanized steel. Below the top where food doesn't directly contact the surface, galvanized construction delivers excellent strength and corrosion resistance at a cost-effective price point.
05
Adjustable Undershelf + ABS Bullet Feet
The undershelf height can be adjusted to fit your storage needs. Adjustable ABS bullet feet level the table on any uneven commercial kitchen floor without scratching tile or concrete.

Who We Are

Built by fabricators

Allstrong Restaurant Equipment Inc. has manufactured high-performance Chinese cooking equipment since 1982. From single-burner wok stations to full custom kitchen builds, every unit is fabricated in-house from premium stainless steel and finished to NSF/ANSI standards.

We sell factory-direct — no middleman — which means better pricing, faster custom turnarounds, and a team that actually knows how the equipment is made. Restaurants nationwide rely on Allstrong ranges, steamers, work tables, and fabrication.

Built-in Sink Bowl — Left or Right Position
The WT-PS Series combines a commercial work table with an integrated sink bowl — eliminating the need for a separate hand sink at the prep station. Available in left-hand (L) and right-hand (R) configurations. Choose EB-Models with galvanized base for cost efficiency, or PB-Models with all-SS base for maximum hygiene.

The WT-PS Prep Table Series is built for stations that need both prep space and washing capability in one unit. 16-gauge stainless steel top with 4" rear upturn reinforced with welded-on hat channel and sound-deadened between top and channels. Gussets recessed into channel to reduce lateral movement. Rolled edge construction. Adjustable ABS bullet feet. Available in 48", 60", and 72" lengths with left-hand or right-hand sink bowl placement. ETL Certified.

Construction Details

Prep & Wash
In One Unit

01
Integrated Sink Bowl
The sink bowl is fully integrated into the table — not a drop-in. Welded seamlessly into the top surface with no gap between bowl and table. Left-hand (L) or right-hand (R) placement ordered at time of purchase.
02
4" Rear Upturn — All Around
4" rear upturn runs the full length of the table — including behind the sink bowl area. Smooth radius at the intersection protects the wall from both prep splatter and sink overspray.
03
Hat Channel + Sound Deadening
Top reinforced with welded-on hat channel. Sound-deadened between top and channels. Gussets recessed into the channel to reduce lateral movement — same construction quality as the premium work table series.
04
EB or PB Base — Your Choice
EB-Models use galvanized legs, undershelf and gussets — cost-effective. PB-Models use stainless steel throughout — for wet environments or when health code requires all-SS construction below the top.

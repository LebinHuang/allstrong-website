16-GA SS Top
Polished 16-gauge stainless steel food-contact surface. Heavier gauge than economy models — less flex, less denting, longer service life. Corrosion-resistant and easy to sanitize to meet health code requirements.

Hat Channel + Sound Deadening
Welded-on hat channel reinforcement is installed beneath the top before sound-deadening material is added between the top and channels. Eliminates the hollow resonance of bare sheet-metal tables during commercial prep work.
WeldedSound DeadenedAnti-Resonance

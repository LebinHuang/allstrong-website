Anatomy

01
16-GA 304 SS Top + Legs
16-gauge 304-grade stainless steel throughout — top and legs. Polished food-contact surface with welded-on hat channel reinforcement. Sound-deadened between top and channels for quiet operation.
02
Aluminum Cross Bracing
Unique to the all-SS open-base design: aluminum cross rails tie the four legs together for lateral rigidity. Eliminates wobble without an undershelf, while keeping the base fully open for cleaning and access.
03
Recessed Gussets
Gussets recessed into the hat channel — not bolted externally. Reduces lateral movement between legs and top structure. Structural rigidity without exposed hardware that collects debris.
04
Open Base — Full Access
No undershelf means unrestricted access under the entire table. Maximises airflow, simplifies floor cleaning, and lets you place mobile equipment or bins anywhere beneath the work surface.
05
Adjustable ABS Bullet Feet
Four adjustable ABS bullet feet level the table on any uneven commercial kitchen floor. Corrosion-proof, floor-safe, easy to clean. 72"+ models include 6 legs as standard.

Engineering Breakdown

How It's Built

01
🏗️
16-Gauge 304 SS Top
The polished 16-gauge 304-grade stainless steel top provides a durable, corrosion-resistant work surface. Heavier gauge than economy models — less flex, longer service life, better hygiene.
02
🔩
Aluminum Cross Bracing
Unique to the all-SS open-base design: aluminum cross bracing ties the legs together beneath the top for lateral rigidity. Eliminates wobble without the need for an undershelf.
03
🎩
Welded Hat Channel + Sound Deadening
The top is reinforced with welded-on hat channel framework beneath the surface. Sound-deadening material between the top and channels absorbs vibration and reduces noise during prep work.
04
🔗
Recessed Gussets
Gussets recessed into the hat channel reduce lateral movement between legs and top. Provides structural integrity without external bracing that could catch debris.
05
✂️
Rolled Edge Construction
All four edges of the table top feature smooth rolled-edge construction — no sharp corners. Prevents injuries, creates a professional finished look, and simplifies cleaning.
06
🦶
Adjustable ABS Bullet Feet
Adjustable bullet-style ABS feet level the table on any uneven commercial kitchen floor. Won't scratch tile or concrete, easy to clean, and won't rust or corrode.

Material Science

Forged in Stainless

Every Allstrong unit starts as a mill-finish sheet of premium stainless steel — laser-cut, brake-formed, and TIG-welded by hand to tolerances measured in thousandths of an inch. No stamped corners. No exposed fasteners. Just precision metal, built to outlast the kitchens it lives in.

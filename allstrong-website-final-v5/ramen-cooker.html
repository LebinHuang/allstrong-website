The details that
keep service moving.

Purpose-built components for filling, cooking and draining, with service controls at the front of the unit.

  • 01

    Custom water tank with a 6, 9 or 12-hole plate for individual noodle cooking baskets.

  • 02

    One 23 or 32-tip jet burner, with standard output of 130,000 or 140,000 BTU/hr depending on configuration.

  • 03

    Manual faucet control lets the operator maintain the preferred water level.

  • 04

    Lower front ignition controls with a Baso-H15DA-1 safety valve and thermostats; pilot and gas shut-off valves for servicing.

  • 05

    1¼″ water drain with cleanout located inside the lower front panel.

  • 06

    Natural Gas or Propane configurations with an included ¾″ regulator.
